# Phase 3 — Commander-Specific Rules

(Assumes Phase 2 is complete.)

- Designate a commander for Commander-format decks.
- Compute the commander's **full color identity** (all mana symbols in cost *and* rules text, including symbols on the reverse face for double-faced cards) and restrict card selection in that deck to cards within that identity.
- Enforce singleton + the 100-card exact size + Commander banned list (distinct from other formats' banned lists).
- Optional, lower priority: support Partner commanders and Backgrounds (both relax the "one commander" rule in specific, rules-defined ways).
